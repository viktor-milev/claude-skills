## v1.3 — June 2026

First public release. A first-principles, plain-English company-comprehension
skill: a twelve-section memo that decodes what a listed company does, how it
makes money, who pays and why, and whether it is a good, mediocre, or fragile
business — with every science or engineering term translated into a faithful
plain-English analogy (the de-jargoning discipline). Two modes — **Deep** (emits
a Phase-1 Research Brief to run with the Research feature, then ingests the
dossier) for obscure or technically hard names, and **Standard** (single-pass
inline retrieval) for well-covered ones. A "character, not magnitude" numbers
line keeps valuation out; a Markdown memo is always produced, with an on-demand
"Field Notes" HTML report. Published directly at v1.3; v1.0–v1.2 were private
working versions. See the inline CHANGELOG in `SKILL.md` for the full v1.0→v1.3
design history.
